Process

How we work.

Every project follows the same four-stage structure. You send a brief — we handle everything from that point forward.

No vendors to coordinate. No technical decisions on your end. No equipment to source, no locations to arrange. We are the single point from brief to delivery.
01
📋 Brief & Script
We start by understanding your objective, audience, tone, and key messages. If you don't have a script, we write one. If you do, we refine it. This stage is done in days — not weeks.
Approved script, scene structure, and voiceover direction
02
🎨 Image & Visual Generation
Custom visuals built around your brand identity — not stock libraries. Environments, scenarios, and scene compositions that would be impractical or impossible to shoot on location.
Visual direction review before full production begins
03
🎬 Video Generation
Motion, voiceover, and cinematic composition produced with AI — calibrated for the context your video will be seen in, whether that's a boardroom, broadcast, or digital campaign.
Motion sequences ready for assembly and editorial
04
✂️ Curation & Final Edit
Human-led editing, pacing, sound design, colour grade, and quality pass. This is where raw AI output becomes a finished, broadcast-ready video. Not a template — a crafted piece of communication.
Final video · MP4 / MOV · Broadcast & digital ready

About

Who we are.

Belvorich is a Jakarta-based AI production agency. We produce corporate video content for mid-to-large companies that need professional output — without the traditional production house timeline, logistical complexity, or access constraints.

We are not a software tool. Every project is written, directed, curated, and delivered by people who understand brand communication and corporate context. The AI is the engine. The judgement behind it is human.

Our starting point is always the same: what does this video need to communicate, to whom, and in what context will it be seen? The technology we use to answer that question is less important than getting the answer right.
